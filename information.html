About HERTS

HERTS is a wearable thoracic monitoring system designed to provide real-time information about changes in the chest through Electrical Impedance Tomography (EIT).

The system uses a wearable vest with electrodes placed around the chest. Electrical measurements collected from the body are processed to generate a visual representation of the thoracic region. HERTS also integrates ECG monitoring to provide additional information about cardiac activity.

HERTS is designed to make thoracic monitoring more practical and easier to understand through a connected web-based platform.

How HERTS Works

HERTS combines wearable hardware, data acquisition, signal processing, and web-based visualization in one monitoring system.

  1. Wear the HERTS Vest

    Put on the HERTS vest and make sure the electrodes are properly positioned around the chest.

  2. Connect the Device

    Connect the HERTS device and make sure the system shows that the device is ready for monitoring.

  3. Start Monitoring

    Start a monitoring session through the HERTS platform. The system collects thoracic measurements and ECG data during the session.

  4. View the Monitoring

    The collected data is processed and displayed through the HERTS platform, allowing users to view thoracic activity and ECG information in real time.

  5. Review Your Session

    After monitoring is completed, the session is saved so users can review the monitoring history and session details.

What You Can See

During a monitoring session, HERTS provides several types of information:

  • Thoracic VisualizationView changes in the thoracic region through EIT.
  • Heart ActivityView cardiac electrical activity through ECG.
  • Device StatusCheck whether the device and connection are ready for monitoring.
  • Monitoring HistoryReview previous monitoring sessions and their recorded information.

Who Can Use HERTS?

Patients

Use HERTS to participate in a monitoring session and review your previous monitoring information through the platform.

Doctors & Healthcare Professionals

Access patient monitoring information and review thoracic and cardiac data through the doctor interface.

Hospitals & Clinics

Use the HERTS platform as part of a connected environment for wearable thoracic monitoring.

Important Information

  • HERTS is designed as a monitoring system and its displayed information should not be interpreted as a standalone medical diagnosis.
  • Monitoring results may depend on proper placement of the vest and electrodes.
  • Make sure the device is properly connected before starting a monitoring session.
  • Poor electrode contact or movement during monitoring may affect the quality of the collected data.
  • If the device shows a connection or signal issue, check the device and electrode placement before continuing the session.
