What We Offer

HERTS brings wearable technology to thoracic monitoring, providing real-time information in a practical and easy-to-understand way.

  • Real-time thoracic monitoring using Electrical Impedance Tomography (EIT).
  • Visual insights into the chest and heart activity through integrated EIT and ECG monitoring.
  • Wearable and practical design for a more convenient monitoring experience.
  • Monitoring history at your fingertips, allowing users to review previous sessions through the HERTS platform.
